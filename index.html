⚠️ QR Security Awareness

You just scanned a QR Code!

Don't worry. 😊 This is a Cyber Security Awareness Demonstration designed to educate you about QR-related security threats.

🤦 Common Mistakes We Make

❌ Scanning a QR Code immediately because it says “FREE”.

❌ Not checking where the QR Code came from.

❌ Not checking the URL after scanning.

❌ Quickly opening an unknown website.

❌ Immediately trusting messages such as “Congratulations! You Won!”.

❌ Entering passwords, OTPs or PINs without verification.

❌ Providing bank or card details without verifying the website.

❌ Taking immediate action when a message creates urgency.

🛡️ Before Scanning a QR Code

1️⃣ STOP – Think twice.

2️⃣ VERIFY – Check where the QR Code came from.

3️⃣ CHECK – Carefully check the website/domain that opens.

4️⃣ PROTECT – Never provide passwords, OTPs, PINs or banking details to suspicious sites.

5️⃣ REPORT – If something looks suspicious, report it to your IT/Security team.

🔐 Remember

A QR Code is only a link.

A QR Code does not automatically mean that the destination is safe.

Cyber criminals can use QR Codes to redirect users to phishing websites and fake offers.

Next time you see a QR Code:

🛑 STOP → 🤔 THINK → 🔎 VERIFY → ✅ THEN SCAN
